Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to 成立最強門派 (the “Application”), developed by 阿煙 (“we,” “us” or “the developer”). It explains how data is handled in the Application and when you contact us.

Effective / last updated: October 18, 2026 Package: com.RAstu.wuxiaschool Contact: friyyer94@gmail.com

Your game progress is stored on your device.
The current version has no advertisements, analytics tracking, account sign-in or cloud-save service.

1. Information We Handle

Local game saves and preferences

The Application stores your sect name, game progress, character and item data, facility layout, and preferences such as audio volume in its storage on your device. This data is used to restore your game and apply your settings. The Application does not automatically upload this data to us.

Local diagnostic records

At startup, the Application creates a diagnostic record on your device. It may include the startup time, device model, memory capacity, graphics processor information, graphics API, loading stages, memory usage and error messages. It is used to troubleshoot loading issues. It does not contain account data or game-save contents and is not automatically sent to us.

Information you send to us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots or diagnostic information you choose to attach. We use this information only to answer questions, provide support, handle privacy requests and investigate issues you report. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unrelated to your request.

The Application does not require your real name, phone number or account information. It does not use advertising identifiers to track users, and does not request access to precise location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

2. Use and Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or use in-game data for personalized advertising or cross-application tracking. The current version does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics tracking, account sign-in or a developer-operated cloud-save service.

Game saves and diagnostic records remain on your device. Emails you voluntarily send are processed through email service providers so that we can receive and reply to them. If disclosure is required by law, we handle and disclose information only to the extent necessary to meet that obligation.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Game saves and settings: These normally remain until you clear the Application’s data or uninstall it. On Android, open Settings → Apps → 成立最強門派 → Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes your local game progress.
  • Startup diagnostics: The local record is normally overwritten when the main menu appears on the next launch. Clearing app data or uninstalling also removes records stored in the Application’s storage.
  • Support emails: We retain them only as necessary to handle your inquiry, provide support, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations, and delete them when the purpose is complete and retention is no longer needed. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of information you voluntarily provided by contacting us below. We handle requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

There is no online account to delete. If your operating system or device services enable backup, restore or data transfer, those services control the retention and deletion of their backups. This is separate from an in-game cloud-save service. Removing local app data may not remove system backups.

4. Security

Local data relies on the application-storage protections provided by your device’s operating system. We limit the use of support information we receive to the purposes described above and take reasonable measures to protect access to it. No device-storage or electronic-transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and email account.

5. Platform Services and This Website

Google Play, Android device services and email services may process information related to downloads, device diagnostics, system backups or communications under their own features and settings. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy and your device service provider’s information for their practices.

This policy webpage contains no advertising, analytics tracking scripts, forms or cookies set by us. When served through GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, GitHub, may process connection information such as IP addresses, browser information and request times to provide and protect its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not require users to create an account or provide personal identity information. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has sent inappropriate personal information to us by email, please contact us. We will help review the matter and handle or delete that information in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy describes the current features of the Application. We may update it when features or legal requirements change and will revise the date shown above. If advertising, analytics, sign-in or cloud services are added in the future, we will update the disclosures before the relevant data processing begins and provide notices or obtain consent where required.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, data handling or privacy requests, contact the developer:

阿煙
friyyer94@gmail.com

You may use “成立最強門派 — Privacy Inquiry” as your email subject to help us identify your request.

隱私權政策

本政策適用於開發者「阿煙」提供的《成立最強門派》，說明遊戲及您主動聯絡我們時的資料處理方式。

生效及最後更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日 套件：com.RAstu.wuxiaschool 聯絡：friyyer94@gmail.com

遊戲進度儲存在您的裝置。
目前版本無廣告、無分析追蹤、無帳號登入，也未提供雲端存檔服務。

01我們處理哪些資料

遊戲存檔與偏好設定

為保存遊玩進度，本遊戲會在裝置的應用程式儲存空間記錄門派名稱、遊戲進度、角色與物品資料、設施配置，以及音量等設定。這些資料用於恢復遊戲狀態與套用您的偏好，不會由本遊戲自動上傳給我們。

本機診斷記錄

遊戲啟動時會在裝置內留下診斷記錄，內容可能包含啟動時間、裝置型號、記憶體容量、圖形處理器資訊、圖形介面、載入階段、記憶體使用量及錯誤訊息。此記錄用於排查載入問題，不包含帳號資料或遊戲存檔內容，也不會自動傳送給我們。

您主動提供的聯絡資料

若您透過電子郵件聯絡我們，我們會收到您的電子郵件地址、郵件內容及您自行附上的截圖或診斷資料。這些資料僅用於回覆詢問、提供支援、處理隱私權請求與排查您回報的問題。請勿寄送與問題無關的敏感個人資料。

本遊戲不要求提供真實姓名、電話或帳號資料，不使用廣告識別碼追蹤使用者，也不要求取得精確定位、通訊錄、相機或麥克風權限。

02資料使用與分享

我們不出售使用者的個人資料，也不將遊戲內資料用於個人化廣告或跨應用程式追蹤。目前版本沒有廣告 SDK、使用行為分析追蹤、帳號登入或開發者營運的雲端存檔服務。

本機遊玩資料與診斷記錄由裝置保存。您主動寄送的郵件會透過電子郵件服務商處理，以便我們接收與回覆；如依法有義務提供資料，我們僅於法律要求的必要範圍內處理。

03資料保存與刪除

  • 遊戲存檔與設定：通常保留至您清除應用程式資料或解除安裝為止。您可前往 Android「設定 → 應用程式 → 成立最強門派 → 儲存空間」，選擇清除儲存空間或清除資料；實際名稱可能因裝置而異。這會移除本機遊戲進度。
  • 啟動診斷記錄：通常於下一次啟動並顯示主頁時覆寫。清除應用程式資料或解除安裝也會移除應用程式儲存空間內的記錄。
  • 聯絡郵件：僅在處理詢問、支援、爭議或履行法律義務所必要的期間保存；目的完成且不再需要保留時予以刪除。您可透過下方 Email 要求查詢、更正或刪除您主動提供的資料，我們會依適用規定處理。

本遊戲沒有可供刪除的線上帳號。若您的作業系統或裝置服務啟用備份、還原或資料移轉，相關備份的保存與刪除由該服務管理；這與本遊戲提供雲端存檔不同。刪除本機資料不一定會一併刪除系統備份。

04資料安全

本機資料使用裝置作業系統提供的應用程式儲存空間保護機制。我們將主動收到的支援資料限制於處理問題所需用途，並採取合理的存取保護措施。不過，任何裝置儲存或電子傳輸方式都無法保證絕對安全，請妥善保護您的裝置與電子郵件帳號。

05平台與本政策網站

Google Play、Android 裝置服務及電子郵件服務可能依其各自功能與設定處理下載、裝置診斷、系統備份或通訊相關資料。這些服務的處理方式請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及相關裝置服務商的說明。

本政策網頁本身沒有加入廣告、分析追蹤程式、表單或自行設定 Cookie。若本頁透過 GitHub Pages 提供，GitHub 作為網站託管服務商，可能處理 IP 位址、瀏覽器資訊及請求時間等連線資料，以提供及保護服務。詳情請參閱 GitHub 隱私權聲明。

06未成年使用者

本遊戲不要求使用者註冊帳號或提供個人身分資料。若家長或監護人認為未成年人透過聯絡郵件向我們提供了不適當的個人資料，請聯絡我們，我們會協助確認並依適用規定處理或刪除。

07政策更新

本政策適用於目前描述的遊戲功能。我們可能因功能或法規變更更新本政策，並調整頁面上方的更新日期。若未來新增廣告、分析、登入或雲端服務，將於相關資料處理開始前更新說明，並在適用情況下提供必要通知或取得同意。
