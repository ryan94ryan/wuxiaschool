Privacy Policy

This policy applies to 成立最強門派 (the “Application”), developed by 阿煙 (“we” or “the developer”). It describes local gameplay, optional save transfers and information you voluntarily send to us.

Effective / last updated: October 24, 2026Package: com.RAstu.wuxiaschoolContact: friyyer94@gmail.com

Game progress is normally stored on your device. If you choose to use Data Transfer, Firebase processes the information necessary to upload or retrieve a save using a one-time transfer code. The Application does not include advertising or behavioral analytics tracking. You do not need to register or sign in with a personal account to play.

1. Information We Handle

Local game saves and preferences

The Application stores sect and character names, gameplay progress, character attributes, equipment, inventory, currency, facility layouts, gameplay records and preferences such as language and audio volume on your device. These records restore gameplay and apply your settings. Ordinary gameplay does not automatically upload your game save to our transfer database.

Optional save transfer

From the main menu settings, you may choose Data Transfer. Generating a code uploads a snapshot of the supported game-save data, including player-entered names, game progress, characters and items, currency, facility layouts, gameplay records, language and completion status. The transfer also includes the app/save version and information used to validate the save.

The transfer database processes the save, a brief save summary, creation time, a hashed transfer-code reference, anonymous authentication identifiers and claim/request records used to control access, prevent duplicate claims and resume interrupted transfers. Generating a code does not automatically update the uploaded snapshot with later progress.

When you generate a code, look up a save or import a save, Firebase Authentication creates or reuses a pseudonymous authentication identity. This background technical identity does not require your name, email address, phone number, password or Google account. Authentication credentials are stored in the Application’s private device storage to support later verification and retries; they are not included in the transferable game-save bundle.

You can play without using Data Transfer. Uploading requires the in-app confirmation. Looking up or importing a code also requires a network connection and the authentication described above.

Connection and security information

Firebase may process IP addresses, user-agent strings and operational/security records to authenticate requests and prevent abuse. The Application does not use these records for personalized advertising or derive a location profile from them.

Local diagnostics

A local startup diagnostic record may contain launch times, device model, memory capacity, graphics information, loading stages, memory usage and errors. It is used to troubleshoot loading. It is not automatically sent to us and is not part of the save-transfer bundle.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, provide support and handle privacy inquiries. Please avoid sending unrelated sensitive information.

The Application does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access, and does not use advertising identifiers to track users.

2. Purposes and Service Providers

Transfer information is used to move the save you selected between devices, preview the save, verify its integrity, manage access and interrupted requests, and prevent a code from being claimed more than once. We do not sell personal information or use transfer data for advertising or cross-application tracking.

We use Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database as service providers for these functions. The transfer database is configured in Singapore (asia-southeast1). This does not mean that all related processing occurs there: Firebase Authentication operates from U.S. data centers, and other service operations may involve locations described by Google. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Email providers process communications you send us. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Local saves, settings and credentials: Normally remain until you clear app data or uninstall. On Android, use Settings → Apps → 成立最強門派 → Storage → Clear storage/Clear data. Names vary by device. This removes local progress but does not by itself delete information already held by Firebase.
  • Unclaimed transfer saves: Remain until a successful transfer or cancellation; there is currently no automatic expiration period. Before a code has been claimed, its originating device can use “Cancel This Transfer Code” while connected to the network to remove the uploaded save.
  • Successful transfers: After a verified local import and successful completion confirmation, the transfer record’s save payload, summary, authentication identifiers and claim metadata are removed. If completion is interrupted, data may remain until the receiving device completes the retry.
  • Used-code markers: A hashed-code reference with a used-status marker remains to prevent reuse. It contains no save payload, sect name or authentication user ID. It currently has no automatic expiration.
  • Anonymous authentication records: These are separate from the transfer record and are not automatically deleted when a save is transferred, a code is canceled or the app is uninstalled. They remain available for authentication and retries until separately deleted. Firebase’s operational logs and backup handling follow its own retention and deletion practices.
  • Local startup diagnostics: Normally overwritten when the main menu appears on the next launch. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes records in app storage.
  • Support emails: Retained only as needed to handle the inquiry, support, disputes or legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed.

For privacy inquiries, including requests about access, correction or deletion, contact friyyer94@gmail.com. We may need limited information to locate the relevant record and confirm the request concerns your data. We will explain any identification or retention limits and handle requests under applicable requirements. The Application currently does not provide a single in-app control that deletes all Firebase authentication and operational records.

Uninstalling the old device’s game is not a cancellation request. Operating-system backups are managed separately by the relevant device/backup service; removing local data may not remove those backups.

4. Security

Transfer and authentication requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. Local records rely on the operating system’s app-storage protections, and database access is restricted by authentication and access rules. This is not end-to-end encryption: authorized administrators and service providers may process the data to operate the service. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

A transfer code is an access secret. Anyone who obtains it may be able to retrieve the associated save. Keep it private; do not post it publicly or send passwords or authentication tokens in support messages.

5. Platform Services and This Website

Google Play, Android backup/device services, external websites and email services process information under their own features and policies. Opening an external link may expose connection information to the destination website.

This policy webpage contains no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or cookies set by us. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process connection information to deliver and protect the website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Children and Privacy Inquiries

Ordinary gameplay does not require personal account registration. The optional transfer feature processes the data described above when used. If a parent or guardian has a concern about a minor’s data or information sent to us, please contact us so we can review the matter and handle it under applicable requirements.

7. Policy Updates

We update this policy when data-handling practices or relevant requirements change and revise the date above. Where required, we provide additional notices or obtain consent before new processing begins.

隱私權政策

本政策適用於開發者「阿煙」提供的《成立最強門派》，說明本機遊玩、選用的存檔轉移功能，以及您主動聯絡我們時的資料處理方式。

生效及最後更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日套件：com.RAstu.wuxiaschool聯絡：friyyer94@gmail.com

遊戲進度平時儲存在您的裝置。只有選擇「轉移資料」功能時，才會由 Firebase 處理上傳或領取存檔所需的資料。遊戲沒有廣告或使用行為分析追蹤；一般遊玩不需要註冊或登入個人帳號。

一、我們處理哪些資料

本機存檔與偏好設定

遊戲會在裝置內記錄門派與角色名稱、遊玩進度、角色屬性、裝備、倉庫、金幣、設施配置、遊玩紀錄，以及語言與音量等設定，用於恢復遊戲狀態及套用偏好。一般遊玩不會自動把遊戲存檔上傳至我們的轉移資料庫。

選用的存檔轉移

您可在主選單設定選擇「轉移資料」。產生轉移碼時，會上傳當時支援的遊戲存檔快照，包含自行輸入的名稱、遊戲進度、角色與物品、金幣、設施配置、遊玩紀錄、語言及通關狀態，以及應用程式／存檔版本和驗證資料。產生後的新進度不會自動同步到這份快照。

轉移資料庫會處理存檔、存檔摘要、建立時間、轉移碼的雜湊索引、匿名驗證識別碼，以及領取／請求紀錄，用於控制存取、防止重複領取及恢復中斷的轉移。

當您產生轉移碼、查詢或匯入存檔時，Firebase Authentication 會建立或沿用背景的匿名驗證身分。這項技術身分不需要您的真實姓名、Email、電話、密碼或 Google 帳戶。裝置的應用程式儲存空間會保留驗證憑證，以支援後續驗證與重試；憑證不會放入可轉移的遊戲存檔包。

不使用轉移功能仍可正常遊玩。上傳前會由遊戲顯示說明並要求您確認；查詢及匯入轉移碼同樣需要連網，並使用上述匿名驗證。

連線與安全資料

Firebase 可能為驗證請求及防止濫用而處理 IP 位址、使用者代理字串及服務運作／安全紀錄。本遊戲不將這些紀錄用於個人化廣告，也不據此建立使用者的位置檔案。

本機診斷紀錄

啟動診斷紀錄可能包含啟動時間、裝置型號、記憶體容量、圖形資訊、載入階段、記憶體使用量及錯誤訊息，供排查載入問題。此紀錄不會自動寄送給我們，也不包含在存檔轉移包內。

聯絡資料

若您寄送電子郵件，我們會收到您的 Email、郵件內容及自行附上的檔案，用於回覆、支援與處理隱私權詢問。請避免提供與問題無關的敏感資料。

本遊戲不要求精確定位、通訊錄、相機或麥克風權限，也不使用廣告識別碼追蹤使用者。

二、用途與服務供應商

轉移資料用於將您選擇的存檔帶至另一台裝置、預覽及驗證存檔、管理存取與中斷請求，並防止同一轉移碼被重複領取。我們不出售個人資料，也不將轉移資料用於廣告或跨應用程式追蹤。

本功能使用 Google 的 Firebase Authentication 與 Firebase Realtime Database 作為服務供應商。轉移資料庫設定在新加坡（asia-southeast1），但不代表所有相關處理都在新加坡：Firebase Authentication 由美國資料中心提供，其他服務作業亦可能涉及 Google 說明的處理地點。詳情請參閱 Firebase 隱私權與安全說明及 Google 隱私權政策。

聯絡郵件會由電子郵件服務商處理。若依法有義務揭露資料，我們僅在必要範圍內處理。

三、保存與刪除

  • 本機存檔、設定與憑證：通常保留至清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。Android 可至「設定 → 應用程式 → 成立最強門派 → 儲存空間 → 清除儲存空間／清除資料」操作，名稱依裝置而異。這會移除本機進度，但不會自動刪除 Firebase 已保存的資料。
  • 尚未領取的轉移存檔：保留至成功轉移或主動取消，目前沒有自動到期時間。在代碼尚未被領取前，原裝置可連網使用「取消此轉移碼」移除已上傳的存檔。
  • 已完成的轉移：接收裝置驗證並匯入存檔、成功確認完成後，轉移紀錄中的存檔內容、摘要、驗證識別碼與領取資料會移除。若完成確認中斷，可能保留至接收裝置重試完成為止。
  • 已使用代碼的標記：會保留雜湊索引與已使用狀態，防止代碼再次使用。此標記不包含存檔內容、門派名稱或驗證使用者 ID，目前沒有自動到期時間。
  • 匿名驗證紀錄：與轉移存檔是不同資料。轉移完成、取消代碼或解除安裝，不會自動刪除 Firebase Authentication 內的匿名驗證紀錄；這些紀錄會保留供驗證與重試使用，直到另行刪除。Firebase 的服務作業日誌與備份另依其保存及刪除規則處理。
  • 本機啟動診斷：通常於下次啟動並顯示主頁時覆寫。清除應用程式資料或解除安裝也會移除應用程式儲存空間內的紀錄。
  • 聯絡郵件：僅於處理詢問、支援、爭議或法律義務所需期間保留，不再需要時刪除。

若有資料查詢、更正或刪除相關請求，請聯絡 friyyer94@gmail.com。我們可能需要有限資訊來定位相關紀錄及確認資料歸屬，並會說明無法識別資料或依法需保留等限制，依適用要求處理。目前遊戲沒有可一鍵刪除所有 Firebase 驗證與服務作業紀錄的功能。

刪除舊裝置上的遊戲不等於取消轉移。作業系統備份由相關裝置／備份服務管理；刪除本機資料不一定會刪除這些備份。

四、資料安全

轉移與驗證請求使用 HTTPS 進行傳輸加密。本機資料受作業系統的應用程式儲存空間機制保護，資料庫另以驗證及存取規則限制權限。這不是端對端加密，授權管理者與服務供應商可能為提供服務而處理資料。任何儲存或傳輸方式都無法保證絕對安全。

轉移碼是存取憑據，持有者可能領取對應存檔。請妥善保存，不要公開張貼，也不要在支援郵件中提供密碼或驗證權杖。
